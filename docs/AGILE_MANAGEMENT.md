# Agile Project Management & Quality Assurance Hub

This directory contains the complete Agile artifacts, defect tracking records, and unit test plans for the **AI-Guided Academic Project Progress Tracking Platform with Planning & Mentorship Assistance**, developed over a **3-Month Lifecycle (May 27, 2026 – August 24, 2026)** across **6 Agile Sprints** by a 4-member cross-functional engineering team.


---

## 📊 Summary of Agile Artifacts

### 1. [Agile_Template_v0.1.xlsx](file:///d:/ai-academic-project/Agile%20Document/Agile_Template_v0.1.xlsx)
The master Agile tracking workbook comprising 4 structured worksheets:
* **`Product Backlog`**: 20 User Stories (US-01 to US-20) prioritized using MoSCoW methodology with dependencies, assignees, and completion statuses.
* **`Sprint Backlog`**: 37 granular engineering tasks (T-101 to T-606) across Sprints 1–6 with task dates, member allocations, activity types (`Design`, `Coding`, `Build`, `Unit Test`, `Integration Test`, `System Test`, `Regression Test`, `Others`), original estimates (4 to 12 hrs), and Day 1–14 burndown effort curves.
* **`Stand up Meeting`**: 20 daily standup logs capturing impediments (e.g. Gemini rate limits, Mermaid syntax parsing, Pinecone vector dimension mismatch) and actionable resolutions.
* **`Retrospection`**: 14 sprint retrospective entries detailing Start Doing, Stop Doing, Continue Doing, and Action Taken.

### 2. [Defect_Tracker Template_v0.1.xlsx](file:///d:/ai-academic-project/Agile%20Document/Defect_Tracker%20Template_v0.1.xlsx)
* **`Defects`**: 18 documented defect tickets logged across Sprints 1–6 categorized into `Logical`, `User Interface`, `Maintainability`, and `Standards`.
* **Defect Density & Resolution Rate:** 100% of detected defects remediated, verified, and closed with regression test links.

### 3. [Unit_Test_Plan_v0.1.xlsx](file:///d:/ai-academic-project/Agile%20Document/Unit_Test_Plan_v0.1.xlsx)
* **`UT`**: 22 comprehensive unit test cases covering authentication, student onboarding, the 7 LangGraph agent nodes, Pinecone RAG semantic search, dynamic plan adjustment, on-demand academic document generation, faculty dashboard analytics, and Groq fallback routing.
* **Pass Rate:** 100% (22/22 test cases passing).
